02 · The creative

One layout. Six messages. Every slot cut from the same file.

This is the part that keeps the cost at zero and the brand consistent. There is one master layout. Six messages ride it. Two hero states, a photograph or a flat panel. Every other format, the Reel, the carousel, the Stories, the square, the link preview, the cover, is generated from that same master by script. When you change a line, everything downstream regenerates in one run. Nothing is designed twice and nothing is paid for twice.

How that $650 splits by job
40% Reels60% feed and carousel

About $260 on Reels, bought on a video views objective. Cheap reach, and it is what fills the retargeting pools we own afterward.

About $390 on feed posts and carousels, bought on traffic straight to the /dscr wizard. This is the half that produces landing views and started applications.

Geography is Ohio, South Carolina, Georgia, Texas, Florida and North Carolina. The budget is not split evenly across them. Lending ads sit in a restricted category, so we cannot target by interest or income, which means the creative does the filtering and the money follows whichever post is working, wherever it is working.

What that money actually buys

Page and profile engagersEveryone who touched the Page or the Instagram account in the last 365 days.
Video viewersEveryone past 25% and past 75% of a Reel. The strongest signal we can get without a form.
Site visitors180 days of everyone who landed on paritylending.com.
Wizard starters30 days of everyone who opened /dscr. The warmest list we will have.

Those four pools are the asset. Sixty days of small money fills them, and once each is past roughly a thousand people, retargeting them is a fraction of the cost of reaching a cold investor. That is why tier one looks so small. We are buying the cheap version of the audience before we buy volume.

The rules the money runs under

QualifyTop third hook rate on Reels, or top third click rate on feed posts, measured at 48 hours. Below that, no money.
KillClick rate under half the account average after $30 spent, or the wrong audience showing up in the comments. Money moves to the next post the same day.
ReadHook rate, thruplay, link click rate, cost per landing view, wizard starts. At this budget, cost per lead is a guardrail, not a scoreboard.
