03 · Targeting

You can't target investors. The creative does it.

Every paid Parity post is a Financial products and services ad on Meta. That strips age, gender, ZIP, interests, lookalikes and exclusions. What's left is states, a 15-mile radius floor, and audiences built from people who already stopped on the content.

Not available

  • Age or gender narrowing
  • ZIP codes, location exclusions
  • Interest targeting for "investors"
  • Lookalike audiences
  • Custom audiences as hard limits (Advantage+ overrides them)
  • TikTok or LinkedIn ads for this product

What we use

  • Six pilot states: OH · SC · GA · TX · FL · NC
  • Creative that self-selects investors
  • Video-view, Page and IG engagement audiences, built from day one
  • Pixel + Conversions API on paritylending.com; Lead fires on the /dscr wizard
  • Every click lands on /dscr, never an instant form
  • Verification prompt? Stop and review before answering.

Rules

Qualify: top-third hook rate (Reels) or CTR (feed) at 48 hours.

Objective: Reels → video views (fills the audience). Images and carousels → traffic to /dscr.

Kill: CTR under half the account average after $30, or the wrong audience in the comments.

Read: hook rate, thruplay, link CTR, cost per landing view, wizard starts. Cost per lead is a guardrail, not a metric, at this budget.

05 · Test discipline

One variable at a time. Act only on big effects.

Message

M1–M6, one per week. Read on hook rate, CTR, wizard starts per 1,000 reach.

Hero state

Photo A vs flat B, alternated by week. Act only if one wins by 50%+ across three messages.

Layout challenger

Month 2 or 3, only if the master is flat. Carries the winning message and state so layout is the only variable.
